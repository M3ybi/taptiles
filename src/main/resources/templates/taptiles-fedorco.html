How to play

Rules and scoring

Connect Pick two equal numbers with a straight, one-turn, two-turn, or outside-edge route.
Score Regular hits add 10 points. Misses subtract 5. Selecting a tile does not change score.
Streak Consecutive hits add +1 extra point per streak step, capped at +10.
Grade Grades compare your score with the board's possible score and adjust by difficulty.
Failed board If no solution remains, the board is blocked and you can retry the same setup.
Tutorial Practice mode shows possible routes and does not affect score.
